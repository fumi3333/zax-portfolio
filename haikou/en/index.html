Closed schools offered for reuse in Japan

Municipalities in Japan publish closed public schools that they want to sell or lease, mostly as Japanese PDFs. This page turns the Ministry of Education's list into a searchable English table: 423 properties, with land area, building age, seismic standard and zoning where available. Each entry links to the municipality's own page and the original document.
